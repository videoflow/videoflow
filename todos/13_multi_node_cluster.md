videoflow emits hostPath volumes and nothing else — no PVCs, no StorageClass, no accessModes anywhere in the repo (manifests.py:420-426). work_dir is resolved at compile time and baked into node params, so host, node, and pod must agree on one absolute path. On a multi-node managed cluster, pods of the same flow land on different nodes and simply don't see each other's output. That is the real barrier to EKS/GKE/AKS, not the k8s version.
